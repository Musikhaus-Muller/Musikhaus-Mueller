`exact_price` means one official set or product page prices the exact requested offer.
`complete_component_calc` means every required component is identified, quantity-matched,
and priced from an official public PRO LIGHTING product page. Multi-item calculations
are marked `collection_disabled` because a single product URL cannot safely represent
the cart. `incomplete_recipe` is never published as a partial total. `no_public_evidence`
is qualified as `no_candidate` or `no_source`; a candidate rejected for a subtype or
model mismatch is called out in the row notes.
